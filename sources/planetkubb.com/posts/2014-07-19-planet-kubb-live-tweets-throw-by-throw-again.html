Planet Kubb Live Tweets Throw-by-Throw Again

For the 2nd year in a row Planet Kubb has used it’s notation system to share with the world throw-by-throw action from the finals match of the US National Kubb Championship! People from around the world were able to follow the game in real time, regardless of country or language. Planet Kubb is the first and only venue to ever live tweet throw-by-throw Kubb matches.
