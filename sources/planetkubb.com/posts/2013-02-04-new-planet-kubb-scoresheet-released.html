New Planet Kubb Scoresheet Released

Jamie scored 8 amazing championship games at the Loppet and sent over some notes on how to improve the Planet Kubb Scoresheet. Namely, increasing the space for freeform comments and having a proper box to indicate any advantage line. I took a good hard look at the overall structure of the page and dramatically cleaned it up. The box to indicate and advantage line (in meters from the baseline) now connects the inkast box and the baton boxes (as it should). The Winning Team designation and Time End line were relocated to the top of the page. These minor adjustments opened up so much vertical space that not only are there 2 full lines for freeform commenting on each turn - but there's now support for 11 rounds (which at ~3 minutes per round means you'll probably get called on time before you need a second scoresheet ).
