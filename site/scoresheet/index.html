Planet Kubb Scoresheet

One landscape page for one game, with a row per turn and a box per baton, written in the notation.

Download the scoresheet (PDF, v11)

Using it

  • Fill in both teams on the left: each player's name and the identifier you'll use for them. Tick which team opened and which won the king toss.
  • The opening team's turns go in the left column and the answering team's in the right. Each row has a box for the advantage line, a box for the inkast, and six boxes for batons.
  • When a team clears its last field kubb and moves on to the baseline, connect the small gaps between the boxes with a thick line. That makes batons to clear (BTC) easy to see.
  • The numbers above each row (T6, T12, T18 and so on) are the throw count at the end of that turn, so you don't have to count batons to find a throw number.
  • The legend in the bottom left lists every code. The notation page has the details.

The stats at the bottom

Garrick and Jamie picked three numbers in 2013 that capture most of a team's game, and the scoresheet has room to work them out on the pitch:

Efficiency %
First-baton efficiency: sum(F@1) / sum(I), the share of a team's thrown-in kubbs that its first baton of each turn knocks down. High numbers mean the inkaster and blaster work well together.
0B@T#
Zero base kubbs at throw number: the team's throw count when the other team's last base kubb falls. It shows how quickly a team gets through the field to the baseline. Five is the best possible.
Hit %
(T - sum(-)) / T: the share of a team's batons that knock something over. Elite teams are above 50%.

The Stats page works all three out for you, along with batons to clear and the rest.
