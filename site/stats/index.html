Game stats

Paste a game written in Planet Kubb Notation, one turn per line. Headers like [Team A "…"] are optional.

How the numbers are worked out
Hit %
Batons that knocked over a kubb or legally took the king, divided by batons thrown. A missed king (=), an illegal throw (Z) and an illegal king hit count as misses. Unthrown batons (X) don't count. Neither do batons a scorer wrote down after the game had already ended.
Efficiency
First-baton efficiency: field kubbs knocked over by each turn's first baton, divided by kubbs thrown in. It's the scoresheet's sum(F@1) / sum(I).
0B@T#
The team's throw count, across the whole game, when the other team's last base kubb fell.
BTC
Batons to clear: in each turn that started with field kubbs, how many batons it took to knock them all down. This is the average over the turns where the field was cleared.
Targets
Field kubbs and base kubbs on the half being attacked, at the start of the turn. Field kubbs include any the other team failed to clear earlier.
Who threw first
The First header if there is one. Otherwise it's worked out from Win and the turn that took the king, and if that isn't possible Team A is assumed. Turns then alternate.
Warnings
The page replays the game kubb by kubb and points out anything that doesn't add up. For example: an inkast that doesn't match the kubbs knocked down the turn before, a missing advantage marker, a base kubb hit while field kubbs were still standing, or a king taken early. The stats still use what was written.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere. The recorded games come from the Planet Kubb wiki (2011 to 2015), entered by the volunteers who scored them.
