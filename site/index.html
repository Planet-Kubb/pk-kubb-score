Score kubb throw by throw

A short notation for writing down every kubb and every baton in a game, a scoresheet for doing it on the pitch, and a calculator that turns the record into stats.

7. 3I 2F F - B B = # Missed king shot!

That line is one turn: the seventh of the game. The team throws in three kubbs, takes two field kubbs with the first baton and the third with the second baton, misses once, knocks over two base kubbs, then misses the king.

Where this came from

Jamie Thingelstad and Garrick van Buren created the Planet Kubb Notation in 2012. They wanted to keep throw-by-throw records of the US National Kubb Championship, and to know what actually separates elite teams from the rest of us. Over the next few years the notation picked up rescue kubbs, advantage lines measured in meters, and codes for resigned and called games. The scoresheet went through eleven versions.

Volunteers scored games at the US Nationals in Eau Claire, at Midwest tournaments from Minneapolis to Des Moines, and at events in Canada, Germany, Switzerland and Sweden. They entered them on the Planet Kubb wiki between 2011 and 2015. Those 150 games are all here, and you can open any of them on the Stats page.
